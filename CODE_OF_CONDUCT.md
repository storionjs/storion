# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **storion** project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behavior includes:**

- Being respectful and inclusive in language and feedback
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them get started
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Showing empathy toward other community members

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**

- Harassment, trolling, or discriminatory comments (in issues, PRs, or other channels)
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or open-source setting

## How to contribute

- **Bug reports and feature ideas:** Open an issue and describe the problem or idea clearly.
- **Code changes:** Open a pull request against the `develop` branch. Keep changes focused and include a short description.
- **Documentation:** Fixes and improvements to docs are always welcome via PRs.

We appreciate your time and effort. Thank you for contributing.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported (e.g. by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainers). We will do our best to address reports in a timely and fair manner.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (e.g. GitHub repository, issue tracker, discussions) and when representing the project in public.
